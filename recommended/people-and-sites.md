# People & Sites

### [DevOps Toolkit](https://www.youtube.com/@DevOpsToolkit)

Viktor Farcic's YouTube channel. Deep dives into Kubernetes, GitOps, platform engineering, and cloud native tools. No fluff, just solid technical content.

### [DevOps Paradox](https://www.devopsparadox.com/)

Podcast by Viktor Farcic and Darin Pope covering DevOps, platform engineering, and cloud native topics. Great for commutes — informative discussions without needing to watch a screen.

### [Scott Rosenberg](https://vrabbi.cloud/)

Permanent guest on [DevOps Toolkit AMA](https://www.youtube.com/c/devopstoolkit) with vast cloud/Kubernetes/infrastructure experience. His blog covers Crossplane, platform engineering, and advanced Kubernetes patterns. Also behind [TeraSky's open-source Backstage plugins](https://terasky-oss.github.io/backstage-plugins/). Highly recommended.

### [Whitney Lee](https://whitneylee.com/)

Developer Advocate at Datadog who makes complex cloud native concepts accessible. Her whiteboard explainers and talks are some of the best in the space.

### [selfh.st](https://selfh.st/)

The best newsletter for self-hosting news, app discoveries, and community updates. If you run a homelab, subscribe.
